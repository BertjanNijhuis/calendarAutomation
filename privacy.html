Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Calendar Automation is a private automation used only by its owner. It is not available to other users.

What it accesses

  • Read-only access to the owner's own Gmail messages, to find appointments in recent mail.
  • Access to the owner's own Google Calendar, to create events for appointments that are not yet in it.

How the data is used

Message text is read on the owner's own computer and analysed by a text-processing service only to extract appointment details (title, date, time, place). It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else. Nothing is stored beyond the calendar events created and a local log on the owner's computer.

Retention and deletion

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page. Calendar events created by the automation can be deleted in Google Calendar.
